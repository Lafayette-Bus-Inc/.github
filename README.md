## About Us

Welcome to the official GitHub hub for **Lafayette Bus Inc.**! We are a premier school bus transportation group operating on the ROBLOX platform. Our primary experience is meticulously crafted and based in the vibrant city of Lafayette, Louisiana. 

This organization houses the codebases, open-source assets, web infrastructure, and documentation that power our community and in-game operations. We pride ourselves on delivering a highly realistic, engaging, and professional transit roleplay experience for our players.

## What You Will Find Here

- **Game Infrastructure:** Scripts, modules, and systems that power our ROBLOX experiences.
- **Web Services:** Codebases for our application centers, staff dashboards, and API integrations.
- **Documentation:** Guides and handbooks for developers, contributors, and staff members.
